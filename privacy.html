Privacy Policy for Bluebit V3

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Bluebit V3 does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.

Your progress (coins, unlocked items, scores and settings) is saved only on your own device using local browser storage. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

The game loads a pixel font from Google Fonts, which may log a standard request to Google's servers. The Share button uses your device's share sheet only when you tap it.
